Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

I do not use this portfolio to ask for, collect, sell, or share your personal information. There are no analytics, advertisements, tracking pixels, or contact forms.

Who this policy covers

This policy explains how personal information is handled when you visit this portfolio. Dmytro Krasylnikov is responsible for the portfolio content. It does not cover LinkedIn, GitHub, or any other website or service with its own privacy practices.

Information I collect

I do not collect personal information directly through this site. In particular:

  • there are no forms, accounts, comments, newsletters, or payment features;
  • there are no first-party analytics or advertising services; and
  • the site does not set cookies or use browser storage.

GitHub Pages hosting

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs and stores a visitor's IP address for security purposes whether or not the visitor is signed in to GitHub. GitHub, rather than this portfolio, controls that processing. For details about the information GitHub processes, why it processes it, retention, sharing, international transfers, and your choices, read the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Cookies and tracking preferences

This portfolio does not use cookies or tracking technology, so it does not display a cookie banner and does not respond differently to browser “Do Not Track” or Global Privacy Control signals. Your browser and GitHub may still apply their own settings or controls.

External links

The portfolio contains ordinary links to LinkedIn but does not embed LinkedIn content. No request is sent to LinkedIn merely because you load this site. If you follow a LinkedIn link, LinkedIn may receive information from your browser and its own privacy policy applies.

Retention, sharing, and sale

Because I do not collect personal information through this portfolio, I do not retain, share, or sell it. GitHub and LinkedIn determine their own retention and disclosure practices for information they process.

Your privacy choices

There is no personal-information profile or account to access, correct, download, or delete from this portfolio. Requests concerning information processed by GitHub or LinkedIn should be directed to the relevant service using the privacy controls and contact methods in its policy.

Children's privacy

This portfolio is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the site's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when an update is published.

Questions

For questions about this policy or the portfolio's privacy practices, contact me through LinkedIn. Please do not include sensitive personal information in your message.
