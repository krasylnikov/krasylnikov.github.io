Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy describes privacy practices for Dmytro Krasylnikov’s portfolio website at krasylnikov.github.io. It applies to this website, rather than any apps or other services.

Information collected by this website

This is a static website. It does not have accounts, contact forms, analytics scripts, advertising, or embedded third-party content. The website code does not set cookies, use browser storage, or ask you to submit personal information.

Hosting and technical information

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, your browser sends technical information to the hosting provider, such as your IP address and request details. GitHub states that it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. GitHub’s handling and retention of this information are described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

External links and contact

The website links to LinkedIn. Following a link takes you to a separate service whose privacy practices are governed by its own policy. See the LinkedIn Privacy Policy.

If you contact Dmytro through LinkedIn, the information you choose to share will be available through that service. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in your message.

Your privacy questions and choices

You can browse this website without creating an account or submitting personal information. For questions about this website’s privacy practices, or requests concerning information you have shared directly with Dmytro, contact Dmytro Krasylnikov on LinkedIn. For information processed by GitHub or LinkedIn, consult that provider’s privacy statement and contact options.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website’s features or privacy practices change. The date above indicates the most recent update.
